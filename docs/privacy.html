Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Daily Fact Drop Publisher only requests the permissions needed to upload drafts (user.info.basic, video.upload).

We store only the access tokens needed to upload the owner's videos, kept as encrypted secrets. We do not collect, sell or share personal data, followers, messages or any other information from TikTok.

The videos uploaded are original educational content created by Daily Fact Drop.

To delete all data, revoke the app's access in TikTok settings; the stored tokens then stop working and are removed.
